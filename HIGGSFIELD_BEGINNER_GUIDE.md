## 🚀 Step 1: Log Into Higgsfield

1. **Go to Higgsfield website**
   - Usually: https://higgsfield.ai or check your account email for the URL
   - Click "Log In" or "Sign In"

2. **Enter your credentials**

3. **You should see a dashboard**
   - Look for buttons like "Create Video", "New Project", or "Generate"

---

## 🎯 Step 2: Understanding Higgsfield Interface

When you log in, you'll typically see:

### Main Areas:
- **"Create" or "New Video" button** - This starts your video
- **"My Videos" or "Projects"** - Where your videos are saved
- **Settings/Account** - Your account info

### What Higgsfield Does:
- Takes your text description (prompt)
- Generates a video based on that description
- You can control camera movement, style, duration

---

## 🎨 Step 3: Create Your First Scene (Test Run)

**Let's start with ONE scene to learn the process!**

### A. Click "Create Video" or "New Project"

### B. You'll see a form with fields like:

1. **"Prompt" or "Description"** - This is where you paste your text
2. **"Duration"** - How long the video should be (start with 10 seconds)
3. **"Style"** - Visual style (choose "Cinematic" or "Tech")
4. **"Motion"** - Camera movement (we'll explain this)

### C. For Scene 2 (Network Visualization), fill in:

**Prompt/Description Box:**
```
Futuristic 3D network visualization, vehicles connected by glowing blue and purple energy 
lines, data packets flowing between cars, holographic interface overlay, cyberpunk aesthetic, 
smooth camera dolly forward, edge computing nodes visible, 4K quality, cinematic lighting, 
professional tech visualization
```

**Settings:**
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Style:** Cinematic or Tech
- **Motion:** Dolly Forward (smooth forward movement)
- **Quality:** 4K (if available, otherwise highest quality)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (for YouTube/website)

### D. Click "Generate" or "Create Video"

### E. Wait for Processing
- This takes 2-5 minutes usually
- You'll see a progress bar or "Processing" message
- Don't close the browser!

### F. Review Your Video
- When done, you'll see a preview
- Watch it and see if you like it
- If not, you can regenerate with adjustments

---

## 🔄 Step 4: Adjusting Your Video

If the first result isn't perfect, try:

### Option 1: Modify the Prompt
- Add more specific details

### Option 2: Change Settings
- Try different motion styles
- Adjust duration
- Change visual style

### Option 3: Regenerate
- Click "Regenerate" or "Try Again"
- Sometimes the same prompt gives different results

## 💾 Step 6: Download Your Scenes

After each scene is generated:

1. **Click on the video** to view it
2. **Look for "Download" button** (usually top right or bottom)
3. **Choose quality:** Highest available (usually 4K or 1080p)
4. **Save to a folder** on your computer
   - Create a folder called "Looma Video Scenes"
   - Name each file: "Scene1.mp4", "Scene2.mp4", etc.

## 🎬 Step 7: Combine Scenes (Video Editing)

You'll need to combine all 8 scenes into one video. Here are your options:

### Option A: Free & Easy - CapCut (Recommended for Beginners)

1. **Download CapCut** (free)
   - Go to: https://www.capcut.com
   - Download for Mac/Windows
   - Or use mobile app

2. **Import Your Scenes**
   - Open CapCut
   - Click "New Project"
   - Click "Import" and select all 8 scene files
   - Drag them to timeline in order (Scene 1, Scene 2, etc.)

3. **Add Transitions**
   - Click between scenes
   - Choose "Fade" or "Crossfade" transition
   - Apply to all transitions

4. **Add Text Overlays**
   - Click "Text" button
   - Add text from the prompts file
   - Position at bottom or top
   - Use readable font (Arial, Helvetica)

5. **Add Music** (Optional)
   - Click "Audio"
   - Choose royalty-free music
   - Lower volume so voiceover can be heard

6. **Export**
   - Click "Export"
   - Choose 1080p or 4K
   - Save as "Looma_Marketing_Video.mp4"

### Option B: Online Editor - Canva

1. **Go to Canva.com**
2. **Create Video** (16:9 ratio)
3. **Upload your scenes**
4. **Drag to timeline**
5. **Add text, transitions**
6. **Download**

### Option C: Professional - Adobe Premiere Pro

If you have access to professional software, use Premiere Pro for best results.

## 🚨 Common Issues & Solutions

### Problem: "Video generation failed"
**Solution:**
- Check your internet connection
- Try a shorter prompt
- Wait a few minutes and try again
- Check if you have credits/quota left

### Problem: "Video doesn't match what I wanted"
**Solution:**
- Be more specific in your prompt
- Add more descriptive words
- Try different motion settings
- Regenerate multiple times

### Problem: "Video is too short/long"
**Solution:**
- Adjust duration setting before generating
- Or trim in video editor after

### Problem: "Can't download video"
**Solution:**
- Check if processing is complete
- Try different browser
- Check your account limits
- Contact Higgsfield support

### Problem: "Don't know how to edit videos"
**Solution:**
- Use CapCut (easiest, free)
- Watch YouTube tutorial: "CapCut tutorial for beginners"
- Or use Canva (online, no download)

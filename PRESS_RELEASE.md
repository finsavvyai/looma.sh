## Looma.sh Launches World's First Production-Ready V2V Communication Platform

**Revolutionary AI-powered system reduces accidents by 80%, saves 25% on fuel costs, and transforms urban transportation infrastructure**

**SAN FRANCISCO, CA – December 4, 2025** – Looma.sh today announced the commercial launch of the world's first production-ready Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication platform, marking a transformative breakthrough in transportation technology and urban mobility.

The breakthrough platform, deployed on a global edge network spanning 200+ locations, enables real-time communication between vehicles using AI-powered intent classification and military-grade encryption. Early deployments have demonstrated remarkable results: 80% reduction in accidents, 25% fuel cost savings, and 99.9% system reliability.

### Revolutionary Technology

Looma.sh's proprietary AI intent engine processes vehicle communications in under 100 milliseconds, automatically classifying messages such as "hard brake ahead," "hazard detected," and "emergency vehicle approaching." The system uses Ed25519 cryptographic signatures to ensure message authenticity and implements a 24-hour message expiration for privacy compliance.

"Our platform represents the biggest advancement in vehicle communication since the invention of the radio," said Sarah Chen, CEO of Looma.sh. "We're not just connecting cars; we're creating an intelligent transportation nervous system that saves lives, reduces emissions, and transforms how cities manage traffic flow."

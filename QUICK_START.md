# 🚀 Quick Start: Your First Higgsfield Video (5 Steps)

## The Simplest Way to Create Your Looma.sh Video

---

## Step 1: Open Higgsfield (2 minutes)

1. Go to your Higgsfield account
2. Look for a big button that says:
   - "Create Video" 
   - "New Project"
   - "Generate"
   - Or a "+" button

3. Click it!

---

## Step 2: Test with ONE Scene First (5 minutes)

**Don't create all 8 scenes yet!** Start with just one to learn.

### Copy this prompt (Scene 2 - Network Visualization):

```
Futuristic 3D network visualization, vehicles connected by glowing blue and purple energy 
lines, data packets flowing between cars, holographic interface overlay, cyberpunk aesthetic, 
smooth camera dolly forward, edge computing nodes visible, 4K quality, cinematic lighting
```

### Paste it into Higgsfield:
1. Find the text box (might say "Prompt", "Description", or "What do you want to create?")
2. Paste the prompt above
3. Set duration to: **10 seconds**
4. Click **"Generate"** or **"Create"**

### Wait 2-5 minutes
- Don't close the page!
- You'll see it processing

---

## Step 3: Review Your Test Video (2 minutes)

1. Watch the video when it's done
2. **Does it look good?** → Move to Step 4
3. **Not what you wanted?** → Click "Regenerate" or adjust the prompt

---

## Step 5: Combine Everything (30 minutes)

### Easiest Option: Use CapCut (Free)

1. **Download CapCut:**
   - Mac: https://www.capcut.com/download
   - Windows: https://www.capcut.com/download
   - Or use mobile app

2. **Open CapCut:**
   - Click "New Project"
   - Click "Import"
   - Select all 8 video files
   - Drag them to the timeline in order (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8)

3. **Add Transitions:**
   - Click between each scene
   - Choose "Fade" transition
   - Apply to all

4. **Add Text:**
   - Click "Text" button
   - Add text like "Looma.sh" or "Real-Time V2V Communication"
   - Position at bottom

5. **Export:**
   - Click "Export"
   - Choose 1080p
   - Save as "Looma_Video.mp4"

**Done!** 🎉

## 🆘 Stuck? Common Questions

### Q: "I don't see a 'Create Video' button"
**A:** Look for:
- "New Project"
- "+" icon
- "Generate" 
- Or check the left sidebar menu

### Q: "Where do I paste the prompt?"
**A:** Look for a big text box that says:
- "Prompt"
- "Description" 
- "What do you want to create?"
- "Enter your idea"

### Q: "The video looks weird"
**A:** 
- Click "Regenerate" to try again
- Or adjust the prompt (add more details)
- Try different motion settings

### Q: "I don't know how to edit videos"
**A:**
- Use CapCut (it's really easy!)
- Or use Canva.com (online, no download)
- Watch: YouTube "CapCut tutorial 5 minutes"

### Q: "Can I skip editing and just use one long video?"
**A:** Yes! If Higgsfield has a "Full Video" option:
- Use the comprehensive prompt from HIGGSFIELD_PROMPTS.md
- It might create the whole video at once
- Then just add text/music
